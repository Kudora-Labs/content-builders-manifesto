# Kudora Builders’ Manifesto

*A compass, not a manual. These are the beliefs we will not trade away—even if tools, processes, or fashions change.*

---

## Our Aim

We are building a humane digital commons where **recognition for real contribution** is the primary currency. We want a place where people earn dignity, voice, and opportunity by creating value that others can build on.

---

## Our Convictions (the things we won’t compromise)

1. **Human dignity first.** People are never a metric. Work exists to serve people, not the other way around.
2. **Ethics as infrastructure.** Principles are load-bearing, not decoration. We hardwire them into how we operate.
3. **Recognition over wealth.** Capital can accelerate; it cannot replace contribution. Voice and trust flow from verified impact.
4. **Transparency builds trust.** Decisions, credit, and change should be auditable and explainable.
5. **The commons compounds.** Reuse, composability, and open knowledge make everyone faster. Private wins that drain the commons are a net loss.
6. **Many kinds of work create value.** Code, design, education, stewardship, community, and operations all matter.
7. **Difference is fuel.** Divergent views—handled with respect—create better systems than conformity ever will.
8. **Security is care.** Protecting users, funds, and privacy is a moral responsibility, not a speed bump.
9. **Long horizons.** We choose decisions we’d be proud of in ten years, not just ten days.
10. **Accountability without ego.** We own our outcomes, fix harm quickly, and share credit generously.

---

## Our Principles (how conviction becomes behavior)

- **Contribution defines voice.** Influence is earned through work that others adopt, not through status or loudness.
- **Attribution is non-negotiable.** We name the people and projects we build on.
- **Clarity over cleverness.** Plain language, clear intent, explicit constraints.
- **Open by default.** We discuss, document, and decide where others can learn from it—except when safety requires privacy.
- **Fit differences before you split.** When we disagree on methods, we compare evidence, not egos. Forks are a last resort.
- **Build for reuse.** Ship patterns and examples that make the next team faster.
- **Teach what you learn.** Knowledge hoarded is value denied to the commons.
- **Fair play, no gaming.** We design incentives that resist manipulation and reward real outcomes.
- **Brand clarity.** Users must always know what is official, what is community, and what is independent.
- **Care for newcomers.** Welcoming people is everyone’s job; no contribution is “invisible work.”
- **Privacy with purpose.** Data is handled with restraint and respect.
- **Measure to improve, not to rank.** Metrics are instruments, not scoreboards.

---

## Our Boundaries (lines we won’t cross)

- **No deception.** No dark patterns, fake consensus, or hidden backdoors.
- **No extraction without reciprocity.** If you benefit from the commons, you owe it care.
- **No harassment or discrimination.** Ever.
- **No pay-to-win.** Money doesn’t buy recognition or rewrite outcomes.
- **No brand confusion.** We won’t mislead people about affiliation or endorsement.

---

## Our Promise to Each Other

- **We will welcome you.** Your first contribution matters. We’ll make it visible and help you succeed.
- **We will disagree with respect.** We steelman each other’s views, then decide and move.
- **We will credit fairly.** Upstream is named; integration and teaching earn real recognition.
- **We will protect users.** When security is at stake, safety beats speed.
- **We will keep the door open.** Even when paths diverge, we leave room to reconcile.
- **We will learn in public.** Wins and wounds both become lessons for the next builders.

---

## The Recognition Ethic

**Kudos** is how we signal verified impact. It is **earned, not bought**, and it **never replaces humanity**. Recognition exists to make good work visible, foster trust, and guide responsibility—nothing more, nothing less.

---

## The Five Questions (our decision lens)

1. **Dignity:** Does this choice increase people’s agency and respect?
2. **Commons:** Does it grow reuse and shared capability?
3. **Truth:** Is it transparent and explainable to those affected?
4. **Safety:** Does it protect users, funds, and privacy?
5. **Time:** Would we be proud of this ten years from now?

If we can’t answer “yes” to most of these, we change the plan—or we don’t ship.

---

## What This Is (and isn’t)

This Manifesto is a **foundation**, not a framework. Playbooks, processes, and tools may evolve with circumstances. **These values do not.** When mechanisms drift, we use this document to steer back to who we are.

---

> I will put people before metrics, ethics before convenience, and contribution before clout.

> I will build in ways that make others stronger.

*In Kudora, your work doesn’t just count—it compounds.*
